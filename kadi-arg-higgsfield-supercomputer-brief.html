3. The BOYD Live-Ops & Autonomous Generation Architecture

The interactive layer is not a concept. Its backend is already system-designed as an API-first n8n orchestration (Narrative Graph, Player State, Timeline DBs). Higgsfield's tools plug directly into this architecture as first-class generation providers.

BOYD (The Sovereign Orchestrator): The central n8n state-machine managing the Narrative Graph DB and Timeline consistency. BOYD routes generation tasks via a versioned REST API.

Higgsfield Genjutsu (The Pixel-Consistency Layer): The absolute visual enforcer. When BOYD requests a new Kadi card iteration or ARG media drop, Genjutsu ensures the artifact geometry, character facial locks, and palette doctrines remain absolute across thousands of generations.

GPT Astra (The Technical Operator): OpenAI's computer-use agent autonomously operates Blender for 3D artifact prep (Burning Spear, Ring) and Unreal Engine for on-site ARG kiosk previs, feeding conditioning frames to the generative tier.

MiniMax H3 Max (The Rapid Engine): Routed via fal.ai, H3 Max handles high-speed, low-latency generation for weekly digital CCG drops and rapid ARG puzzle media.

Higgsfield Supercomputer & MCP (The Interactive Builder): BOYD leverages Higgsfield MCP to generate prompt-to-playable interactive puzzle rooms. The Supercomputer autonomously plans and renders full-stack ARG experiences from narrative nodes.
