Studio Nothing Elephant · Confidential Partnership Brief

Put your brand inside the story.

Fixed to the Power of None is a two-part cinematic experience: a traditional first-half film followed by a 38-week interactive investigation that repeatedly revisits the same night, progressively revealing the truth.

FILM RIGHTS · LOOP RIGHTS · INVESTIGATION RIGHTS

The structure

HALF 1 — THE FILM

A conventional cinematic narrative establishes the world, characters, conflict and pivotal night.

HALF 2 — THE 38 LOOPS

The same night is revisited week after week. Each loop reveals, contradicts or recontextualizes what the audience thought it understood.

This is not a 38-episode retelling. It is a recurring investigative narrative layered onto the second half of the film.

What a brand can buy

FILM RIGHTS

World, location and character integrations within the traditional cinematic half.

LOOP RIGHTS

Association with selected weekly iterations of the second half.

INVESTIGATION RIGHTS

Evidence drops, branded clues, audience prompts and sponsored reveals.
